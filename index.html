What I Do

The short version is below. The long version comes out when we talk.

Backend Architecture

Figuring out how pieces fit together before anyone writes code. Or honestly, how they should have fit together after someone already started.

Data and Security

Structuring data so the right queries are natural and the wrong ones are impossible. Implementing designs that eliminate entire classes of bugs and attack surfaces.

Security enforced by the database itself. Error handling that assumes things will fail because they do fail. Extensive Tests that expose production bugs before shipping.

Messaging at Scale

SMS and email pipelines that move fast and reliably. When a second of latency per message means an hour across a campaign, the architecture stops becomes the product.

Systems Programming

Writing Rust for things that need to be secure, fast, reliable and correct at the same time.

The best work comes from being honest about what a system knows and what it doesn't, what it can do and what it can't, and what it should do and what it shouldn't.

I'm Christopher. I build backend systems, mostly things that move data around at scale. Right now that means a multi tenant multi channel messaging platform handling SMS and email delivery across thousands of campaigns. aside from that, i also build websites for small bussiness and SME's that need to be online fast, reliably and cost efficiently.

My work is focused on building systems that do the right thing, even when the wrong thing is attempted. Systems that do not need constant monitoring, and do not need to be babysat. Systems that are secure by structure, tested by design, and correct by construction.
